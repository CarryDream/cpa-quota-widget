Composition/framing: square image, one centered app icon. A dark charcoal rounded-square app tile fills about 90% of canvas width, with genuinely transparent area outside its rounded silhouette. The floating card emblem fills about 65% of the tile, optically centered, with enough internal padding. The tile itself is solid charcoal so the off-white emblem remains visible on both light and dark desktops.
Constraints: deliver the icon alone, one design only, no presentation sheet, no comparison layout, no device or desktop mockup, no text, no letters, no numerals, no percent signs, no watermark. Flat fills only; no gradient, no glass, no 3D, no bevel, no texture, no glow, no cast shadow. Preserve real transparency outside the tile; do not depict a checkerboard.
